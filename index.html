SEEN 000

    Log a sighting

    Saved on this device only; it isn't sent to eBird. If you later submit it to eBird and re-import, Ave·Dex recognizes it (same species and date) and doesn't count it twice.

    Settings

    Bird sounds

    Separate Song and Call buttons use xeno-canto, which needs a free personal API key. Create an account, verify your email, then copy the key from your account page. Without a key, entries play an unlabeled recording from iNaturalist.

    Backup

    Your dex is stored only in this browser. Save a backup file now and then, especially after logging sightings, and use it to move your dex to another device (e.g. Mac → iPhone). Backups don't include your xeno-canto key.

    Getting your eBird data

    eBird doesn't let other apps sign in to your account, so Ave·Dex imports eBird's own exports:

    • Best: ebird.org/downloadMyData emails you a .zip. Unzip it and import MyEBirdData.csv.
    • Quick: the CSV download on your Life List page (no sighting counts).

    Re-importing replaces the imported data. Sightings you logged here are kept.
